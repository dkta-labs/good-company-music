Good Company: music sketches

Early, editable Strudel sketches for the short film. Strudel plays them live in your browser from the code below, using recordings of real acoustic instruments (piano, cello, strings, folk harp, guitar, recorder) and its built-in synths. No pre-rendered audio. The first play downloads the samples; if the first notes are missing, stop and play again.

Edit any number in the controls block, then press Play / update (or Ctrl+Enter / Cmd+Enter in the editor). Ctrl+. stops.
Made with Strudel (AGPL-3.0). Code: github.com/dkta-labs/good-company-music. Nothing here is final or locked to picture.
